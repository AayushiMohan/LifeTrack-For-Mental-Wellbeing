# 🌿 LifeTrack — More Than Living

> *"Your circle shapes your destiny — choose people who are spiritual, ambitious, and growth-driven."*

---

## What is LifeTrack?

**LifeTrack** is a wellness web app designed for people who want to slow down, reflect, and grow. It is not just a website — it is a safe space where you can express yourself, get personalized wellness guidance, and find the motivation to keep going.

Whether you are dealing with stress, anxiety, sleep issues, relationship problems, or just need someone to talk to — LifeTrack is here for you. 🤍

---

## Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 💬 **AI Wellness Chatbot** | Talk about anything — stress, anxiety, sadness, loneliness, anger and more. Get real, caring responses |
| 🌱 **Daily Wellbeing Plan** | Fill a form about your routine and get a personalized morning routine, evening routine, daily habits and affirmations |
| 🔐 **Login & Signup** | Create your own account and be part of the LifeTrack community |
| 📬 **Feedback & Contact** | Not satisfied? Send your thoughts directly via email or reach out on Instagram |
| 📱 **Fully Responsive** | Works beautifully on mobile, tablet and desktop |

---

## Tech Stack

**Frontend**
- HTML5
- Tailwind CSS
- Vanilla JavaScript

**Backend**
- Node.js
- Express.js
- bcrypt (password hashing)
- nodemailer (feedback emails)

**Database**
- MongoDB Atlas
- Mongoose
